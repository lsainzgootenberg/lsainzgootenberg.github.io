About

I'm a recent graduate of the University of Chicago, where I studied Anthropology with honors and minored in English and Creative Writing. My work moves between audio production and writing — oral history interviews, live radio, and cultural criticism.

I was a Mellon Mays Undergraduate Fellow, and I've also worked with asylum-seeking and refugee-status youth through oral history and arts programming. I'm fluent in Spanish and conversational in French and Japanese.
